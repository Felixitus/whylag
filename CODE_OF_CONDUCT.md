# Code of Conduct

WhyLag adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming, be patient with people who are new to troubleshooting or to open source, critique ideas and
not people, and assume good intent. Harassment, insults and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) marked "Code of Conduct", or by the contact listed in the
repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues and may
ban contributors who violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
